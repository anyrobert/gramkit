Gramkit for Instagram

Instagram, on your keys.

Keyboard shortcuts

Click + and press the keys. ? on Instagram shows them.

  • Shortcuts never fire while you type in messages, comments or search.
  • Arrow keys and Space act on a video only while the pointer is over it, so they still scroll the feed and move through posts. On Reels, ↑ and ↓ still move between reels. In Stories, the arrows stay Instagram’s.
